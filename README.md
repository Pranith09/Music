# 🎵 Music Player

A clean, minimalist, 100% offline music player designed for Android (optimized for **Realme Narzo 60 5G** and Android 13/14).

Inspired by the simplicity and clean hierarchy of the golden era of portable MP3 players.

---

## ✨ Features

- **100% Offline**: Plays `.mp3`, `.flac`, `.wav`, `.m4a`, `.aac` directly from phone storage without requiring internet.
- **Folder Browser**: Navigate your raw `/Music` and `/Download` directories directly.
- **Background Playback**: Continues uninterrupted when screen is locked or app is minimized.
- **Notification & Lock Screen Controls**: Rich notification bar with live seekbar, cover art, play/pause, and skip buttons.
- **AMOLED Dark Theme**: Pure deep dark interface crafted for AMOLED displays to save battery.
- **Library Organization**: 4 clean tabs (**Tracks**, **Albums**, **Artists**, **Folders**) + instant search & sorting.
- **Sleep Timer**: Stop music automatically after 15m, 30m, 45m, 60m, or 90m.
- **Headphone & Bluetooth Safe**: Auto-pauses when headphones are unplugged.

---

## 📱 How to Download & Install

1. Open this repository on your phone: [https://github.com/Pranith09/Music](https://github.com/Pranith09/Music)
2. Go to **Actions** -> Tap the latest build run.
3. Scroll to **Artifacts** at the bottom and download **`Music-Android-APK`**.
4. Open the downloaded `.apk` and tap **Install**.
5. Launch **Music**, tap **Allow** for *Music & Audio* storage access, and enjoy!
