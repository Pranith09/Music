# 🎵 Aura Music Player

An ultra-sleek, lightweight, 100% offline music player designed for Android (optimized for **Realme Narzo 60 5G** and modern Android 13/14 devices).

---

## ✨ Features

- **100% Offline**: Plays downloaded `.mp3`, `.flac`, `.wav`, `.m4a`, and `.aac` audio files directly from phone storage.
- **Background Playback**: Keeps playing when the app is minimized or when your screen is locked.
- **Lock Screen & Notification Controls**: Interactive seekbar, album cover art, play/pause, and skip controls right in your Android notification drawer.
- **AMOLED Dark Theme**: Pure deep dark interface crafted for AMOLED displays to save battery.
- **Library Management**: Filter and sort by **Tracks**, **Albums**, **Artists**, **Playlists**, and **Favorites**.
- **Docked Mini Player**: Floating bottom bar with realtime progress indicator.
- **Sleep Timer**: Set 15m, 30m, 45m, 60m, or custom countdown to gently stop playback when you fall asleep.
- **Headphone & Bluetooth Safe**: Automatically pauses when headphones or Bluetooth audio devices are disconnected.

## 📱 How to Download & Install on Your Realme Narzo 60 5G

### Step 1: Download the `.apk`
1. Open this repository on your phone's browser: [https://github.com/Pranith09/Music](https://github.com/Pranith09/Music)
2. Go to **Actions** (or the **Releases** section on the right/bottom).
3. Tap on the latest build run, scroll down to **Artifacts**, and tap **`AuraMusic-Android-APKs`** (or download `AuraMusic-arm64-v8a-RealmeNarzo60.apk` from Releases).

### Step 2: Install the App
1. Open the downloaded `.apk` file from your phone's **Downloads** or **Files** app.
2. If prompted: *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"*, tap **Settings** and toggle **Allow from this source**.
3. Tap **Install**.

### Step 3: Grant Audio Permission
1. Open **Aura Music**.
2. When prompted, tap **Allow** for *Music & Audio* storage access.
3. Your offline songs will automatically be scanned and ready to play!
